    Frequently asked questions

    Will my images lose quality?

    Compression removes details your eye can barely see. At a quality of 75 to 85, the difference is usually invisible while the file is often 50 to 80% lighter. Use the Compare button to check before you download.

    Which format should I choose?

    WEBP is the best choice for websites and is supported by all modern browsers. AVIF is even lighter but slower to create and not accepted by every app. JPG works everywhere, including email and printing. PNG is for logos, screenshots and images with transparency.

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. Compression runs in your browser, on your device. Your images are never sent or stored anywhere.

    Is there a size or file limit?

    There is no limit other than your device's memory. Very large images (over 50 megapixels) can be slow on a phone.
