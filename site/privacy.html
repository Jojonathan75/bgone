Privacy

Your images

Background removal, editing and export are computed directly in your browser. Your images are not sent to a server and are not stored by this site, unless you use the paid HD option described below.

HD images (paid option)

Only if you choose the paid HD download, the image you are editing (without its background mask) is sent to our server and processed by our AI upscaling provider, Replicate (United States). It is used only to create your HD image, is not used for training, and the copy sent to the provider is deleted right after processing (results are erased by the provider within one hour). Payments are handled by Stripe: we never see your card number. We keep your purchase code, your credit balance and the purchase record (amount, date) for accounting.

Technical data

Like any website, the host may keep technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) for security and reliability. This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics at this time.

Browser cache

The AI model is stored in your browser cache to speed up later use. You can remove it by clearing your cache.
