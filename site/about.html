About Poppix

Poppix is a free online tool that removes the background of a photo in a few seconds, then lets you fix the details, add a new background and download the result.

Why we built it

Most background removers ask you to upload your photos to a server, create an account, or pay before you can download a usable file. We wanted the opposite: a tool you can open, use and close in one minute, without handing your pictures to anyone.

How it works

When you open an image, an artificial intelligence model called ISNet analyses it and estimates, for every pixel, whether it belongs to the subject or to the background. This happens inside your browser, on your own device, using WebAssembly. Your photo is never sent to our servers.

The model is downloaded once (about 30 to 80 MB depending on your device), then kept in your browser cache, so the next images are processed much faster.

Automatic results are good but never perfect. That is why the editor includes an eraser, a restore brush, edge smoothing and colour adjustments: you stay in control of the final image.

What it costs

Standard-quality downloads are free and will stay free. The site may show advertising to cover its costs. A paid high-definition option, sold as credit packs with no subscription, is planned.

À propos de Poppix

Poppix est un outil en ligne gratuit qui retire l'arrière-plan d'une photo en quelques secondes, puis permet de corriger les détails, d'ajouter un nouveau fond et de télécharger le résultat.

Le détourage est calculé par un modèle d'intelligence artificielle (ISNet) directement dans votre navigateur : vos photos ne sont jamais envoyées sur un serveur. Le téléchargement standard est gratuit ; le site peut afficher de la publicité pour couvrir ses frais, et une option HD payante par packs de crédits est prévue.

Poppix est un projet indépendant mené par Jonathan Bardavid, en France. Pour nous écrire : page contact.
