    Frequently asked questions

    How much smaller will my PDF be?

    It depends on what is inside. PDFs full of photos or scanned pages often shrink by 70 to 95%. PDFs that contain only text (exported from Word, for example) are already compact: expect anywhere from 0 to 50%, sometimes nothing at all. If the result is not smaller, you get your original file back.

    Will the text stay selectable?

    Yes with the Light, Recommended and Strong levels: only the images are recompressed. Only the Pages as images mode turns each page into a picture.

    Are my files uploaded to a server?

    No. Compression runs in your browser with the open-source Ghostscript engine. Your documents never leave your device.

    Can I compress a password-protected PDF?

    No. Open it with its password and save a copy without protection first, then compress that copy.
