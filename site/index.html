Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Background removal and editing are computed by your browser, on your device. No image is sent or stored by this site.

Why is the first cutout slower?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (about 45 to 90 MB depending on the quality). It is then cached, and the next cutouts are much faster.

The result isn't perfect. What can I do?

Open the Touch up tab: the eraser removes leftover background, the Restore brush brings back parts removed by mistake, and smoothing softens the edges.

Which formats can I download?

PNG (with transparency), JPG and WEBP. Choose PNG to keep a transparent background.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in a recent browser. On phones, the standard quality model is used by default to keep the download small.
