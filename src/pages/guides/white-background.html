White background photos for marketplaces

Many marketplaces ask for a plain white background on the main product image. It looks simple, but a few details decide whether a listing looks professional or gets rejected.

Why white?

A white background puts every product on equal terms in search results, removes distractions and makes colours easy to judge. It also lets the marketplace display your image on its own pages without visual clashes.

Common requirements

Rules differ between platforms and change over time, so always check the current guidelines of yours. The usual expectations for the main image are:

  • A pure white background, often specified as RGB 255, 255, 255. Off-white or light grey may be flagged.
  • The product fills most of the frame, with only a small margin.
  • No text, logos, watermarks, borders or extra props that are not included in the sale.
  • A minimum size, often around 1000 pixels on the longest side, so shoppers can zoom.

Step by step

  1. Open your photo in {{brand}} and let it remove the background.
  2. Fix leftover shadows or missing parts in the Touch up tab.
  3. In the Background tab, choose the white swatch. It is pure white.
  4. Optionally add a light drop shadow so the product does not look pasted on.
  5. Download as JPG with Crop to subject enabled.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Halos: a thin dark or coloured outline is very visible on white. Shrink the edge by one pixel.
  • Grey “white”: photographing on white paper is not enough, it always comes out grey. Replacing the background guarantees real white.
  • Over-editing: do not change the product's colours to make it “pop”. Returns often come from products that look different in real life.

Secondary images

Secondary images usually have more freedom: lifestyle scenes, coloured backgrounds, close-ups. Reuse the same cutout with a different background to keep a consistent look across your listing.
