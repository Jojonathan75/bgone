How to remove the background from a product photo

A clean background makes a product look professional and lets shoppers focus on what matters. Here is a simple workflow that works with a phone and a free tool.

1. Take a photo that is easy to cut out

The quality of an automatic cutout depends mostly on the original photo. A few habits make a big difference:

  • Contrast: put a light product on a darker background, and a dark product on a light one. A white mug on a white table is the hardest case for any AI.
  • Soft, even light: shoot near a window on a cloudy day, or use two lamps on each side. Harsh light creates dark shadows that the AI may treat as part of the product.
  • Sharpness: tap the product on your screen to focus, and hold the phone still. Blurry edges always give blurry cutouts.
  • Space around the object: leave a margin on all sides so no part of the product touches the edge of the frame.

2. Remove the background

Open {{brand}} and drop your photo on the page. The background is removed automatically in a few seconds, directly in your browser. Use the Before / after button to compare with the original.

3. Check and fix the details

Zoom in on the edges. Typical problems with product photos are:

  • Shadows left under the object: use the eraser in the Touch up tab with a soft brush.
  • Parts removed by mistake (a transparent bottle cap, a thin handle): switch to the restore brush and paint them back.
  • Jagged edges: raise the smoothing slider slightly, or shrink the edge by one or two pixels to remove a thin halo of the old background.

4. Choose the right background

Most stores prefer a pure white background for the main image, and a coloured or lifestyle background for secondary images. In the Background tab you can pick a colour, a gradient, a blurred version of the original or your own image. A subtle drop shadow makes a product look grounded instead of floating.

5. Export in the right format

  • PNG if you need transparency, for example to place the product on a banner later.
  • JPG for a final image on a white or coloured background: smaller files, accepted everywhere.
  • WEBP for your own website: even smaller files at the same quality.

Tick Crop to subject to remove empty space around the product, then add even margins if your store requires the product to fill a given share of the image.

Quick checklist

  1. Contrasting background, soft light, sharp focus.
  2. Automatic cutout, then zoom on the edges.
  3. Erase leftover shadows, restore missing parts.
  4. White background for the main image, JPG export.
